Additionally, I was responsible for executing data cleaning and data exploration to ensure data accuracy and completeness.

January 2012 - September 2014

Statistical Analyst

Statistics South Africa

Pretoria, South Africa

As a Statistical Analyst for the Census Operations division, I contributed to strategic planning and development of 9 Census projects, and served as a coordinator and a liaison between various clusters within the organisation, to resolve organisational, logistical and budgeting issues. Furthermore, I was involved in the preliminary phase of the undertaking of these projects, where I was charged with the designing of the questionnaires and data entry systems, was involved the complete enumeration process, and participated in the collection, processing and analysis of data and in the dissemination of results. Additionally, I took part in pre and post project evaluations for these projects, where I conducted operational research to identify opportunities for process improvements in data collection, data processing and data governance in general.

October 2016 - December 2017

My work as a Survey Methodologist for the Methodology, Standards and Research division consisted of working in conjunction with various internal divisions to understand their data requirements, and to develop and implement statistical sample surveys for the collection of the required data. My responsibilities were largely in the preliminary phase of the survey process, I was accountable for the development of sampling methodologies for various economic surveys, and this work encompassed the development and maintenance sampling frames, the drawing of samples and the application of estimation methods to ensure precision of the statistical estimates for the surveys.
